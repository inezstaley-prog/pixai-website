Before you visit

Questions couples ask before they tour.

Do we need a wedding date before we tour?

No. Many couples visit with only a season in mind. We’ll check open dates with you on the tour.

Does a tour cost anything?

No. Tours are complimentary and there is no obligation to book. You’ll leave with a written package guide either way.

How long is the tour, and who can come?

About one hour. Bring your partner, your parents or anyone who helps you decide, up to four guests in total.

How many guests can the estate host?

Up to 180 seated for dinner, and smaller weddings are just as welcome. We’ll show you how each size lays out.

Can we see the terrace in late-afternoon light?

Yes. The 2 pm Saturday tour runs into the softer light, and weekday evening visits can be arranged by request.

When will we hear about pricing?

You’ll get answers on the tour and a written package guide within one business day.

Concept project

VELLUVO: The Tilt. One launch, one continuous experience.

Too often, a great ad sends traffic to a generic landing page that has nothing to do with it. That disconnect can erode trust and cost conversions.

Here, the ad and the landing page are built as one experience. The 11-second film sets the mood, and the page picks up the same movement, storyline and musical score. When the page feels like a natural continuation of the ad, visitors know they are in the right place, and a 24-hour private early access offer is an easy yes. Sound is always the visitor's choice: it starts with a tap and can be turned off at any time.

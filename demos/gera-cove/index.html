Questions

Questions owners ask before they call.

What does it cost?

A monthly fee based on rent collected. We put the exact number in your analysis, so you see it before you decide anything.

Am I locked into a contract?

Your analysis is free and has no strings. If you hire us, the agreement is in writing and we go over how to leave before you sign.

Who approves repairs?

You set the limit. Under it, we handle it. Over it, we call you first with photos and a quote.

How do I get paid?

Rent goes to your account after our fee and any repair costs. It's all on your monthly statement.

How fast can you start?

Once you sign, we can usually begin listing within a few days.
